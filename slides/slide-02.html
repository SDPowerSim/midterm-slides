When Does Speculative Decoding Save Energy?

The trade-off

Draft work adds cost. Verification removes repeated target calls.

Savings depend on the kernel shapes, their hardware cost, and how many draft tokens the target accepts.

Energy per token
(k × draft step + verification step) / (accepted tokens + 1)
Speculative decoding cost model2
Contrast five target-only calls with one speculative round at k equals four. The speculative path only wins when verification amortizes target work across enough accepted tokens.
