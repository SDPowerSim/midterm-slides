Shurui, about 70 seconds. Present two findings from profile-driven simulation. First, draft q equals one takes 4.62 ms versus 10.65 ms for target q equals one, a 2.31 times latency ratio. Second, increasing target q to three, five, or nine processes more query tokens in parallel while latency rises to only 1.03 to 1.05 times the q equals one baseline. The longer-query workloads approximate the main kernel work of verification. Together, fast drafting and low additional target latency motivate speculative decoding as an acceleration strategy worth modeling. These are ASTRA-Sim step latencies based on native A30 kernel profiles.
